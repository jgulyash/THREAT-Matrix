## How to apply

Anyone who meets the qualifications in `GOVERNANCE.md` section 2 may offer to serve.

1. Open an issue titled "Reviewer offer" or "Review board offer". If you prefer not to apply in public, use the private contact route in `SECURITY.md` and say that your message is a governance enquiry.
2. Say which seat and area, summarise your relevant practice or research and any credential, and give the disclosures the conflict-of-interest rule asks for.
3. Do not include details of any real case or person.
4. The maintainer replies within 30 days. Reviewers are seated by the maintainer. Board members are seated by the maintainer until three are seated, and after that by the board.
5. Seats carry no payment today. If that changes it is disclosed under `GOVERNANCE.md` section 8.
6. A seat holder may step down at any time by written notice; the table is updated and the seat shown as vacant.

Being listed here means a person holds a role. It does not mean they endorse the vocabulary, and the project will not describe any seat holder as an endorser.
